01 Who we are

The One People, One Kenya Peace Initiative

A peaceful Kenya is a shared responsibility.

The One People, One Kenya Peace Initiative (OPOKPI) is a national, inclusive and non-partisan platform dedicated to promoting peace, national unity, tolerance, dialogue, responsible citizenship and peaceful coexistence.

We bring together citizens, communities, young people, institutions, organizations and partners around a shared commitment to a peaceful and united Kenya.

What guides us

Peace is more than the absence of violence.

It is built through the choices we make, the conversations we have and the respect we show one another.

✳

Unity

Our commitment

A peaceful Kenya
starts with us.

Peace requires listening. It requires understanding. It requires tolerance and responsible citizenship. Above all, it requires each of us to make a conscious commitment to peaceful coexistence.

We may be different. We may disagree. We may come from different communities and backgrounds. But we are one people, we have one Kenya, and we share one responsibility—to protect peace.
