01Our vision
A peaceful, united and cohesive Kenya where diversity is embraced and every citizen contributes to a culture of peace and non-violence.

This is the future that guides our commitment: a country where people from different communities and backgrounds can live together peacefully, respect one another and work toward common aspirations.

Our mission

Mobilize. Empower.
Build peace.

To mobilize and empower Kenyans to promote peace, dialogue, tolerance, responsible citizenship and national cohesion through civic education, community engagement, youth participation and strategic partnerships.

Our mission recognizes that peace-building is a shared effort. It calls for citizens, communities, young people, institutions and organizations to participate constructively in the life of the country and to choose peaceful ways of addressing differences.

“Our diversity should unite us, not divide us.”

What we stand for

Our values guide every step.

These values describe the principles OPOKPI seeks to promote in its work and in the relationships between people and communities.

VALUE / 01

Peace

We promote peaceful coexistence and reject violence as a means of resolving differences.

VALUE / 02

Unity

We recognize that despite our differences, we share one country and one national destiny.

VALUE / 03

Tolerance

We respect diverse opinions, cultures, communities, beliefs and backgrounds.

VALUE / 04

Dialogue

We encourage communication and constructive engagement as tools for resolving differences.

VALUE / 05

Respect

We promote respect for the dignity, rights and freedoms of every individual.

VALUE / 06

Inclusion

We seek to ensure that every community and generation has an opportunity to participate.

VALUE / 07

Responsibility

We encourage citizens to understand and exercise their responsibilities towards their communities and country.

VALUE / 08

Non-partisanship

The Initiative provides an inclusive platform that does not exist to promote or oppose any political party or candidate.

Why peace matters

Peace is the foundation on which communities grow.

Peace is the foundation upon which communities, economies and nations develop. When peace is threatened, the consequences are felt across society—by families, young people, businesses, schools, communities and institutions.

Building peace therefore cannot be left to one institution or one group of people. Peace is everyone's responsibility.

Our approach

Sustainable peace is built from the ground up.

Our approach combines six connected ways of supporting peace-building and civic participation.

01 / EDUCATE

Educate

Provide knowledge and awareness about peace, citizenship, tolerance and dialogue.

02 / ENGAGE

Engage

Create opportunities for citizens and communities to participate in meaningful conversations.

03 / EMPOWER

Empower

Equip young people and community leaders to become advocates for peace.

04 / CONNECT

Connect

Bring together institutions, communities and organizations working toward common objectives.

05 / MOBILIZE

Mobilize

Build a national network of individuals and organizations committed to peaceful coexistence.

06 / SUSTAIN

Sustain

Develop partnerships and programmes that continue beyond individual campaigns or events.
